Treatment Pathway 02

Depression Care & Recovery Support

Depression is more than temporary sadness. It can impact thoughts, emotions, energy levels, relationships, and daily functioning.

We provide comprehensive assessment, treatment planning, and ongoing support to help individuals recover and regain a sense of purpose and wellbeing.

Clinical Indicators

Symptoms May Include:

• Persistent sadness
• Loss of interest in activities
• Fatigue and low energy
• Sleep problems
• Feelings of hopelessness
• Difficulty concentrating
Clinical Objective

Our Goal:

To help individuals improve mood, restore functioning, and build long-term emotional resilience.

✓ Improve mood
✓ Restore functioning
✓ Build long-term emotional resilience
Treatment Pathway 03

Sleep & Insomnia Treatment

Healthy sleep is essential for mental and physical wellbeing. Persistent sleep difficulties can contribute to stress, anxiety, depression, and reduced quality of life.

We assess sleep-related concerns and provide appropriate treatment recommendations to improve sleep quality and overall health.

Clinical Concerns

Common Sleep Issues:

• Difficulty falling asleep
• Frequent night awakenings
• Early morning waking
• Poor sleep quality
• Excessive daytime fatigue
Clinical Care Focus

Treatment Focus:

We assess sleep-related concerns and provide appropriate treatment recommendations to improve sleep quality and overall health.

✓ Comprehensive sleep-related assessment
✓ Appropriate treatment recommendations
✓ Improved sleep quality and overall health

Treatment Pathway 05

Suicide Prevention & Crisis Support

Suicidal thoughts and self-harm behaviours require immediate attention and professional support.

Our clinic provides compassionate assessment, intervention, and guidance for individuals experiencing emotional crises, helping them access appropriate care and support systems.

Clinical Alert

Warning Signs May Include:

• Feelings of hopelessness
• Social withdrawal
• Self-harm thoughts
• Significant behavioural changes
• Emotional distress
Immediate Support

Compassionate Intervention:

Our clinic provides compassionate assessment, intervention, and guidance for individuals experiencing emotional crises, helping them access appropriate care and support systems.
